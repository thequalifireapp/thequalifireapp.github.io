QUALIFIRE

Same road.
New meaning.

Qualifire splits your everyday rides into sectors and times each one against your own last ten trips on that route. Press start, ride, save. A bit better every day.

Android only. Being tested with a small group, not yet on Google Play.
How it works

One ride sets the route. Every ride after it is timed.

1
Ride your normal route

Bike, run or walk. Ride a road once and, at the end, name where you started and ended. That ride becomes the route.

2
Invisible gates split it into sectors

Lines across your path, placed for you. You never see them. You only feel the moment one falls behind you.

3
Every sector is timed against your last 10

Not a record from one perfect ride, but the rolling window of your last ten rides on that stretch. Recent you, not peak you.

The colours

What each colour means.

  • Purple. Faster than every one of your last ten rides through this sector.
  • Green. Faster than your recent average, but not the best.
  • Yellow. Slower than your recent average.

Your only rival is the last ten versions of you.

Privacy by design

Your rides stay on your phone.

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics.
  • Your location, rides and routes are stored only on your phone.
  • If the app crashes, an anonymous technical crash report can be sent so the bug can be fixed. It contains no location, rides or name.
