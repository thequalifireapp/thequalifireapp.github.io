Qualifire privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Qualifire is an Android app that times your regular rides. It is made by Qualifire, an individual developer in Belgium. Contact: thequalifireapp@gmail.com

The short version

Your rides stay on your phone. Qualifire has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and I never receive your location, your rides or your name. The only thing that can reach me is an anonymous technical crash report.

What the app uses and why

Location. To time a ride, Qualifire uses your precise location while a ride is being recorded. While recording it keeps working when the app is in the background or the screen is off, and it shows a notification so you can see it is on. It does not use your location when no ride is being recorded.

Your rides and settings. Rides, routes, results and settings are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are not uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Exports. If you export a ride (for example as a GPX file) and share it, that file goes where you send it. Qualifire does not send it anywhere itself.

Services the app talks to

Map tiles. The map is loaded from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org). To draw the map, your phone requests map tiles from that server, so the server can see your phone's IP address and which map area you are looking at. These requests do not include your ride data. OpenFreeMap states that its access logs do not contain IP addresses, that it uses no cookies or tracking, and that it collects no accounts or email addresses (openfreemap.org/privacy). Its content delivery network, Cloudflare, may collect some technical information under its own privacy policy.

App updates. The app checks for updates from Expo's servers (expo.dev). Expo states that it collects the device operating system and randomized tokens used to tell whether an update was downloaded. As with any server, your IP address is also visible to it. These requests contain no ride data.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, it can send a crash report to Sentry (sentry.io), which I use to fix bugs. A report contains technical details such as the app version, Android version, device model and the error with its stack trace. It is set up not to include your location, your rides or your name, and it is set up not to store your IP address. Reports are kept for 30 days. Sentry stores them on servers in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).

What I do not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics or tracking of how you use the app.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Your choices and rights

You can stop location use at any time in Android settings (Apps, Qualifire, Permissions), and you can delete everything by uninstalling the app. Because rides stay on your phone, I hold no personal data about you that I could look up. If you are in the EU you have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction and deletion. For anything held in crash reports, write to thequalifireapp@gmail.com and I will help.

Children

Qualifire is not aimed at children and is meant for people 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date above. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
