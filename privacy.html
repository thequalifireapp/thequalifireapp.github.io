Qualifire privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short

Your activities' data stay on your phone. Qualifire has no accounts, no targeted ads and never collects analytics or location data.

What the app uses and why

Location. To time an activity, Qualifire uses your precise location while an activity is being recorded. While recording it keeps working when the app is in the background or the screen is off, and it shows a notification so you can see it is on. Qualifire does not use your location when no ride is being recorded.

Your activities and settings. Activities, routes, results and settings are stored only on your phone. They are not uploaded anywhere and uninstalling the app deletes them. Any exports get saved locally only.

Services the app talks to

Map tiles. The map is loaded from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org). To draw the map, your phone requests map tiles from that server, so the server can see your phone's IP address and which map area you are looking at. These requests do not include your activities' data. OpenFreeMap states that its access logs do not contain IP addresses, that it uses no cookies or tracking, and that it collects no accounts or email addresses (openfreemap.org/privacy). Its content delivery network, Cloudflare, may collect some technical information under its own privacy policy.

App updates. The app checks for updates from Expo's servers (expo.dev). Expo states that it collects the device operating system and randomized tokens used to tell whether an update was downloaded. As with any server, your IP address is also visible to it. These requests contain no activity data.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, it can send a crash report to Sentry (sentry.io), which Qualifire uses to fix bugs. A report contains technical details such as the app version, Android version, device model and the error with its stack trace. It is set up not to include your location, your activities or your name, and it is set up not to store your IP address. Reports are kept for 30 days. Sentry stores them on servers in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).

Applying to join the testing (website)

If you apply to join the Android testing on our website, we collect your email address and your answers to the form, only to choose testers and send you an invitation. The form's data is processed by Google on our behalf. We delete your answers when the testing ends. To have your application removed earlier, write to thequalifireapp@gmail.com.

Your choices and rights

You can stop location use at any time in Android settings (Apps, Qualifire, Permissions), and you can delete everything by uninstalling the app. If you are in the EU you have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction and deletion. For anything held in crash reports, write to thequalifireapp@gmail.com and we will be happy to help.

Children

Qualifire is not aimed at children and is meant for people 18 and over.

Changes

Qualifire is committed to keeping this privacy policy up to date. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
