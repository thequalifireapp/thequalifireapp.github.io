Live racing

Race your earlier rides in real time on the map.

Your earlier rides on this way appear as dots that move at their real pace.

You see live whether you are ahead or behind.

It works with your location only, nothing else.

Get moving

Name your own sport.

Bike, run, walk, e-bike, fast walk, or type your own.

Nothing is pre-loaded; everything is built from the rides you do.

Qualifire is made for human-paced sports, not for driving.

Privacy

All routes and places are stored on your phone.

Your rides and results are stored only on your phone.

No account, no ads, no ad tracking.

Recent you, not peak you.

Compared with your current form.

You are compared with your last nine rides on this way, plus today.

Old rides roll out as new ones come in.

So you race your current form, not a record from months ago.

Challenge yourself

No comparing with other people.

Qualifire is about one thing: making your own repeated routes more fun to ride.

Your rides are the only ones on the map.

By you, for you

One route, several ways of your own.

One route, several ways to ride it: dry or wet, day or night, the short cut or the long way round.

Pick the way before you press START.

Each way keeps its own rides and times.
