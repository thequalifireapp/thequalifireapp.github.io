Live racing

Race your earlier rides in real time on the map.

Your earlier rides on this way appear as dots that move at their real pace.

You see live whether you are ahead or behind.

It works with your location only, nothing else.

Just get moving

All activities allowed.

Bike, run, walk, e-bike, fast walk, or type your own.

Whatever gets you moving can be logged in the app.

Privacy

All routes and places are stored on your phone.

Your rides and results are stored only on your phone.

No account, no ads, no location tracking.

Recent you, not peak you.

Compared with your current form.

You are compared only to your most recent activities.

Old activities roll out as new ones come in.

So you race your current form, not a record from months ago.

Find your own path

No comparing with other people.

Qualifire is a personal app designed to make your daily routes more fun.

You always compare yourself to your past selfs, without outside distraction.

By you, for you

One route, many ways.

Life is not always a straight line, and neither are your routes.

Dry or wet, day or night, the short cut or the long way round: Qualifire keeps up with you.
